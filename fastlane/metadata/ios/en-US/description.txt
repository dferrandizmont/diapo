Your film camera, with a live viewfinder. Free and ad-free.

Diapo is a film camera for your phone: you pick the film before you shoot, see the result in the viewfinder while you frame, and take the photo. It is not a filter app for editing afterwards. It is a camera.

LIVE VIEWFINDER
• Swipe the cartridges to change film and watch the effect in real time: colour, grain, vignette, light leaks and halation.
• The saved photo looks like the viewfinder, at the resolution of your camera.
• Fine-tune each film (amount of grain, vignette and more) and use the flash in auto, on, off or party mode.

TEN FILMS, ALL FREE
• Summer 98: warm colours from a nineties summer.
• Instant: square frame with a white border and washed-out colours.
• Cinema: widescreen, warm skin tones and greenish shadows, with optional bars.
• Cold slide, Expired, Flash night, Classic B&W, Press B&W, Disposable and Toy camera.
No film, format or feature is behind a payment.

ROLLS THAT DEVELOP
Load a roll with a single film and shoot without looking. While the roll lasts you cannot see the photos: they are stored encrypted on your phone. When you close it, choose to develop right away, after a wait or the next morning. Once developed you open it as a contact sheet and can share the whole sheet as one image. You can give each roll a name.

DISPOSABLE CAMERA
A 27-frame camera with one film, no photo screen and no settings, to pass from hand to hand at a dinner or on a trip. The photos develop when the roll is finished. To leave it you have to hold the exit stamp.

DEVELOP THE PHOTOS YOU ALREADY HAVE
Pick photos with the system picker, see how they look with each film and develop them at full resolution. Hold a finger on the photo to see the original.

YOUR OWN GALLERY
Developed rolls and days of loose photos, shown as strips of film. Each photo shows its film and date, and you can share or delete it from there.

CAMERA DETAILS
• Optional date imprint in the style of the old cameras.
• 3:2, 4:3, 1:1 and 16:9 formats.
• Shutter and film-advance sounds, and light vibrations, which you can turn off.
• A level in the viewfinder, also with the phone held sideways.
• Save the untouched original next to the photo, if you want.
• Optional location: it is written only into your photo, and it is off by default.

PRIVACY
• No accounts, no ads and no watermark.
• The app does not connect to the internet: your photos never leave your phone.
• Open source (GPL-3.0): anyone can check this in the code.
• Photos of an undeveloped roll are stored encrypted and do not reach the Photos app until you develop them.
• No analytics and no trackers.
• If you uninstall the app before developing a roll, those photos are lost.

Available in Spanish, Catalan and English.
